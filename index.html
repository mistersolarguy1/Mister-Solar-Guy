Why Go Solar

Going green means saving a different kind of green

Get in touch with a member of our team today to ask how you can save on your system. New York State offers homeowners who switch to solar an investment tax credit equal to 25% of the total installation cost, up to $5,000 — and the NY-Sun Incentive Program offers rebates that allow you to go solar with no money up front.

Switching to solar has environmental, financial, and tax benefits — reducing your reliance on an increasingly expensive grid, and letting you sell excess energy back for credit on your bill.

FAQs

Common questions about going solar

How much can I save by switching to solar?+

Savings depend on your property's energy usage and system size, but New York homeowners can access a state investment tax credit worth 25% of installation cost (up to $5,000), plus NY-Sun rebates — meaning many customers go solar with little to no money upfront.

Do you install roof-mounted and ground-mounted systems?+

Yes. We install roof-mounted systems on asphalt, rolled, and metal roofing, as well as ground-mounted systems — whichever best fits your property and goals.

Can I add a battery or EV charger to my system?+

Absolutely. Batteries, EV chargers, and energy monitoring platforms can all be added to a new or existing solar system for extra resilience and control.

What areas do you serve?+

We proudly serve homeowners and businesses across New York State and beyond, including the Hudson Valley, Albany, Westchester, and surrounding regions.
